# Page heroes — concepts for sign-off (22 Nov 2026)

One world, one grammar. The homepage ends with the camera entering the observatory portal; every other page picks up inside that same world. Shared rules:

- **Look:** night, graphite and silver, one warm ochre light source (the brand accent). Same lens language as the homepage (wide establishing, slow dolly).
- **Mechanic:** scroll drives a single continuous AI shot (generated with the studio's Magnific workflows); one hold where something procedural happens on the exact frame; the page headline lands at the end as the handoff into the content. 3–4 screens on desktop, 3 on mobile, same layout both.
- **Honesty:** anything that implies real work uses real assets (project thumbnails, team portraits). No invented clients, rooms or people.
- **Fallback:** reduced-motion and slow connections get the final frame as a still with the headline.

## 1. Disciplines — "One shot, five hands"
**Frame:** inside the portal: a circular, dark chamber; in its centre a single suspended frame of a raw plate (flat, ungraded, a tracking marker and a rig wire visible).
**Scroll:** the camera circles the frame while each discipline touches it in turn, one hold per hand, procedurally on the exact frame: *Editorial* (the frame slides into a timeline strip and the cut lands) → *VFX* (the wire is painted out, a set extension builds) → *Colour* (a grade sweeps from log-flat to final) → *Conform & mastering* (the frame snaps through 2.39 / 16:9 / 9:16 delivery masks) → *AI-assisted* (noise resolves into the final).
**Handoff:** the finished frame fills the screen; headline "From the first cut to the last detail."

## 2. Work — "The archive"
**Frame:** a long, dark gallery of glass plates suspended in the chamber, each lit from below.
**Scroll:** a slow dolly down the gallery; each plate carries a REAL project still from the archive (composited procedurally onto the plates, not generated), the client name appearing as it passes.
**Handoff:** the camera stops on the newest project; the plates settle into the page's grid.

## 3. Commissioning — "Brief to master"
**Frame:** top-down on a dark desk: a single blank sheet under a warm lamp.
**Scroll:** one continuous morph: the sheet gains handwriting (the brief) → storyboard frames → a timeline → a finished master glowing on a monitor. Each stage lines up with the four process steps on the page (Brief, Proposal, Review in Frames, Delivery).
**Handoff:** the master's light fills the frame; headline "Every film is scoped for what it needs."

## 4. Studio — "Developing"
**Frame:** a lightbox in the dark; the eleven real portraits laid out as a contact sheet, still in negative.
**Scroll:** the portraits develop from negative to positive one by one (procedural, using the real portraits), the ochre loupe passing over each as the name appears.
**Handoff:** the contact sheet becomes the team grid below.

## 5. Contact — "The signal"
**Frame:** the observatory from the homepage, the portal glowing.
**Scroll:** the reverse journey: the camera pulls back out through the portal and across the water to the wide shot, the portal light pulsing like a signal.
**Handoff:** headline "What are you making next?" over the wide shot; the form follows. Bookends the homepage.
