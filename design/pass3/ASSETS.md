## Cinema lens
Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then exported as WebP for the website. Original: lens-reference.png. Web assets: public/images/optics/cinema-lens.webp (1280 square) and cinema-lens-mobile.webp (720 square).

Prompt: Photoreal premium product study of one unbranded cinema prime lens on pure black. Three-quarter view into the front glass, barrel extending upper-right. Black anodized aluminium, three fine-toothed focus rings, silver engraved tick marks without words, brushed metal bevels, nine overlapping iris blades, convincing optical depth. Large softbox reflections, white rim light, subtle fill. Monochrome only. One isolated object, no camera, hands, pedestal, artificial glow, logo or watermark.

## Interactive mesh
The shipped public/models/cinema-lens.glb is a precision reconstruction built by scripts/build-lens.mjs, guided by the generated reference. It is not the rejected Comfy mesh. The source keeps separate black housing, focus gears, engraved ticks, steel bevels, aperture blades and transparent coated glass. The raw Comfy result is retained locally under mesh-output/ for diagnosis and excluded from the deploy.
